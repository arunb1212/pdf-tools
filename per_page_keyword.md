# Per-Page Keyword Targets — English & German

**How to use this doc:** For each tool page, the "Head term" is the high-volume/high-KD term (context only — don't anchor your title on it alone). The long-tail variants are what you should actually target in title/H1/FAQ — lower competition, still meaningful volume, and they map directly to your real differentiator (privacy, no upload, no signup, no watermark).

**Before finalizing:** run every long-tail variant through Google Keyword Planner (set location = Germany for the German column) or Seobility to confirm actual volume/KD — this document tells you _which phrases to check_, not final locked-in numbers.

## The modifier pattern behind every long-tail (use this to generate more)

**English modifiers that consistently lower competition while preserving real intent:**
"without uploading" · "free no signup" · "no watermark" · "online free" · "how to [verb] free"

**German equivalents:**
"ohne Hochladen" · "kostenlos ohne Anmeldung" · "ohne Wasserzeichen" · "online kostenlos"

These modifiers work because they match your actual product differentiator (client-side, no account, no watermark) — you're not just picking easier keywords, you're picking keywords that describe exactly what makes your tool different from the big suites. That alignment matters for conversion, not just ranking.

## Priority order to build/optimize first

1. **CSV to PDF / PDF to CSV** (EN + DE) — best volume-to-competition ratio, your genuine gap
2. **Lock / Unlock PDF** (EN + DE) — less commoditized than merge/split, decent long-tail opportunity
3. **Merge / Split / PDF↔JPG** (EN + DE) — build for completeness and internal linking, target long-tail only, don't expect head-term wins in year one
4. **JPG to CSV, Write, Create, Sign** — validate real volume before investing heavy content effort; these may be low-volume enough that they're better as supporting pages than standalone SEO bets
